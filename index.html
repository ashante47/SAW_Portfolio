Xactimate Mobile eLearning refresh

2026 · Sprint 1Needs analysis + course redesign · Rise 360

A 2018 video-based course had tens of thousands of enrollments and almost no completions. I used LMS data to show leadership that this was a design problem, not a motivation problem, and redesigned the first chapter to prove the new direction.

62.39Klearners enrolled
0.2%completion rate
147total completions
123 minaverage time to complete
The problem
Since 2018, iPads had become the primary device for mobile estimating, but the course hadn’t kept pace. Every lesson was a long, linear video; screen captures showed an outdated interface; lessons ran out of sequence; and learning objectives were vague. Learners enrolled, then left.
Audience
Field estimators and desk adjusters using Xactimate Mobile on iPad, plus customers and partners learning mobile estimating. Most learn between site visits or during an active claim, as just-in-time support rather than a sit-down course.
My role
Led the needs analysis and defined the Sprint 1 strategy for stakeholders, product, and training leadership. Designed and built the redesigned chapter.
Approach
  • Pulled enrollment, completion, and time-on-task data from the LMS and traced the drop-off to four root causes: outdated UI, modality mismatch, poor sequencing, and unclear objectives.
  • Proposed a modality shift and set Rise 360 design standards for image-based walkthroughs, process blocks, and short GIFs for gestures, keeping video only where motion is instructionally necessary.
  • Re-sequenced lessons to follow real mobile estimating workflows and rewrote objectives so each lesson states what the learner will be able to do.
FromWatch a full video to learn a task
→
ToScan visuals → understand the process → apply immediately
Rise 360LMS analyticsNeeds analysisADDIE
Deliverables
A ten-section needs analysis tying each problem to evidence and a proposed fix, with success criteria for the finished course. A redesigned Chapter 2 of nine lessons: six instructional lessons, two Practice It activities, and a Your Turn capstone sketch, each with step-by-step visuals and clear objectives.
Impact
The analysis defined the Sprint 1 scope and secured the redesign direction. Post-update success will be measured against completion rate, time-to-task, learner confidence, support dependency, and learner feedback; results will be added here as the updated course launches.

Xactimate Level 1 Training Guide modernization

Rise 360 · 42 lessonsInstructor-led guide to self-paced digital learning

The Level 1 guide prepares new users for Xactimate certification. I redesigned it from instructor-led materials into a learner-centered digital guide that works across Xactimate Desktop, Online, and Mobile.

The problem
The existing materials were written for a classroom with an instructor present. Learners increasingly needed to prepare on their own, on their own schedule, and the content had drifted from current software workflows.
Audience
New Xactimate users across adjusting, estimating, and contracting, preparing for Level 1 certification. Skill levels range from first-time users to experienced estimators switching platforms.
My role
Redesigned the instructor-led materials into a digital learning experience; updated content to current workflows; wrote the assessments, labs, and supporting documentation; applied accessibility and usability practices throughout.
Approach
  • Organized 42 lessons into seven sections that follow the estimating workflow: getting started, creating a project, interior sketching, roof sketching, writing scope, completing the estimate, and certification prep.
  • Each section opens with a stated outcome and closes with Key Takeaways; a Fast Forward option lets returning users skip to the next topic.
  • Used tabs, accordions, and annotated screenshots to keep dense reference content scannable, with notes on where Desktop, Online, and Mobile differ.
  • Built nine practice estimates (five interior, four roof) so learners apply each skill on a realistic loss before attempting the certification exam.
Rise 360Bloom’s TaxonomyAccessibilityAssessment design
Deliverables
A 42-lesson Rise 360 training guide with embedded practice labs, a certification preparation section, and nine practice estimates. Also published as a 469-page PDF for learners who prefer print or offline reference.
Impact
One guide now serves all three Xactimate platforms and supports both self-paced and instructor-led delivery, replacing materials that had to be maintained separately.

XactRestore eLearning course

eLearning + videoOnboarding for administrators and field technicians

XactRestore is restoration job management software connected to Xactimate and XactAnalysis. I designed its onboarding course and the introduction video that opens it, translating restoration workflows into beginner-friendly learning.

The problem
New customers needed to get administrators set up and field technicians productive quickly, in software that touches estimating, scheduling, documents, and syncing with the claims suite. Restoration processes are complex, and the audience is new to the product.
Audience
Restoration company administrators and field technicians, with distinct needs for estimators, project managers, and business owners.
My role
Designed the onboarding course end to end: course maps, learning objectives, and performance-based assessments. Created the instructional videos, practice activities, knowledge checks, and workflow-based scenarios, partnering with subject-matter experts to get the restoration process right.
Approach
  • Opened with a short “What is XactRestore?” video that answers the learner’s first question, “what’s in it for me?”, by role before any feature training begins.
  • Built scenarios around real jobs (for example, a kitchen water loss) so practice mirrors the field.
  • Paired each workflow with a knowledge check and a performance-based task rather than recall questions alone.
Articulate 360CamtasiaScenario designSME collaboration
Deliverables
The XactRestore eLearning course and its 2:23 introduction video, along with course maps and assessments.
Impact
Gave a new product a structured onboarding path for both the people who configure it and the people who use it in the field. Completion and feedback data will be added as the course matures.

XactGen: Carriers 101

Video · Module 1, Lesson 1.1Introducing an AI-assisted claims workflow

XactGen is a ClaimXperience add-on that turns a policyholder’s guided photos into measurements, a sketch, and an estimate for carrier review. The first lesson had to explain a new, multi-system workflow clearly enough that adjusters trust it.

The problem
The workflow spans XactAnalysis, ClaimXperience, the policyholder’s phone, and Xactimate. Adjusters needed a mental model of the whole process, and of where they act versus where the system acts, before learning any screen.
Audience
Carrier adjusters and claims staff handling qualifying interior water claims.
My role
Designed and produced the module introduction video: the explanatory sequence, visuals, and narration script.
Approach
  • Led with a three-step “what it is” before the detailed six-step workflow, so the simple version lands first.
  • Showed the policyholder’s screen next to the adjuster’s steps to make the hand-off between people and systems visible.
  • Kept the lesson to 2:32, long enough for the full workflow, short enough to finish between tasks.
CamtasiaVideo scriptingProcess visualization
Deliverables
A 2:32, 1080p introduction video for Module 1, Lesson 1.1 of the XactGen: Carriers 101 course.
Impact
Sets up the rest of the module: every later lesson refers back to the six-step workflow introduced here.

LMS experience and administration

OngoingResearch, support, and SCORM delivery for 13K+ learners

Alongside course design, I own much of how learners experience our LMS: the data that tells us what’s working, the support that keeps people moving, and the packaging that lets enterprise clients run our training inside their own systems.

The problem
Thousands of learners across courses, certification exams, learning plans, and access requests, with feedback and support trends scattered across surveys, tickets, and LMS reports.
My role
LMS administration, course maintenance, survey design, and learner support. I analyze feedback, support trends, and LMS data to find learning barriers, and I present findings to design, product, and senior leadership.
Approach
  • Measure success data from courses, exams, and surveys to identify strengths and gaps, then turn findings into data visualizations leadership can act on.
  • Developed and distributed SCORM proxy packages so enterprise customers can deliver and track Verisk training in their own LMS.
  • Established reusable templates and design standards that reduced duplication across learning experiences.
LMS administrationSCORMSurveysData visualization
Impact
Improved training accessibility and completion through timely learner support and assessment administration, and enabled customer organizations to deploy Verisk training inside their own platforms.
